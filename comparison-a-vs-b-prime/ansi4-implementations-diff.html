Where two degradeHexColorToAnsi4() implementations disagree

A · closest hue

Greys have no hue, so they become black below 128 and white from 128. Every other color goes to the nearest of the six hues: red, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta.

B′ · #46944 variant

Greys go to black or white. Below 25% saturation the color becomes black if its brightest channel is under 128, white otherwise. Otherwise each channel is rounded to 0 or 255 on its own and the three bits give the color. Changed from B, which sent every low-saturation color to black.
