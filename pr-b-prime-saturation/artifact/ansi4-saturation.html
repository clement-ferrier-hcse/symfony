Ansi4 with a saturation check: before and after

Each grid is one slice of the RGB cube at a fixed blue level, with 16 levels of red (rows) and green (columns), 00 to ff in steps of 17. Every cell shows the requested color on top, the current result bottom left (each channel rounded on its own) and the result of B′ bottom right. Colors with an HSV saturation under 25% become black if their highest channel is under 128, white otherwise. The others keep the per-channel rounding. Greys, white, black and most pastels give the same result as today, so only a few cells change: muted mid-tone colors that were red, green, blue or similar and now become white.
